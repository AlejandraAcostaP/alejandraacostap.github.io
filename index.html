Research

My MASc work, supervised by Prof. Peter Mascher in the Department of Engineering Physics, connects simulation and experiment to understand radiation damage in structural materials for Generation IV reactors. This research is funded by NSERC.

Radiation damage in reactor steels

Geant4 Monte Carlo simulations of primary knock-on atoms, atomic displacements and displacements per atom (dpa) in conventional and additively manufactured 316 stainless steels.

Positron annihilation spectroscopy

Simulating positron annihilation (PAS and PALS) in irradiated materials, comparing it with laboratory measurements, and building our positron lifetime spectrometer.

Tools: Geant4 (C++), SRIM, Python (NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib), MATLAB, Mathematica, R, Git, Linux.
