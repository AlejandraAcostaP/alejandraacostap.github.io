For students

If you study at a public school or university in Colombia, or anywhere in Latin America, and research abroad feels out of reach: this section is for you. I was there too.

What I wish I had known

  • You don't need perfect English to start. I learned English at university and kept improving it by using it.
  • It's not too late to change paths. I started in sociology before physics, and those years still count.
  • One opportunity opens the next. A 12-week internship led to a scholarship, then to my thesis, a conference and a master's.
  • Caring about your community counts. Leading my school's student council taught me as much as any class.

Programs that took me to Canada

Mitacs Globalink Research Internship. A paid 12-week research internship with a professor at a Canadian university, for undergraduates from partner countries, including Colombia (only some universities are eligible). Students apply in the second half of the year (for 2027 the deadline was September 16, 2026), and internships run between May and October. You need a reference letter from a professor. Official page

Emerging Leaders in the Americas Program (ELAP). A Government of Canada scholarship for students from Latin America and the Caribbean, including Colombia, for a short study or research stay in Canada, at undergraduate, master's or PhD level. The Canadian university applies on your behalf, so you need a host there: an exchange agreement with your university or, for research, a professor who invites you. At McMaster the deadline is in March. Official page

Dates and rules change every year: always check the official pages and ask your university's international relations office.
